# Quality gates

## 1. Source gate

- Preview every chosen source around its intended in and out points.
- Reject frozen openings, temporal jumps, geometry failures, unreadable crops, accidental text, and near-duplicates.
- Confirm the strongest realistic footage appears early.

## 2. Rhythm gate

- Listen once without looking and mark perceived phrase changes and accents.
- Watch once muted and assess visual cadence alone.
- Watch with sound and verify that cuts feel caused by the track rather than placed near it.
- Check every rapid run frame by frame.

## 3. Variety gate

- Build contact sheets from representative times.
- Confirm adjacent shots differ meaningfully in subject, scale, motion, or world.
- For multi-panel sections, compare successive beats and confirm actual source changes.
- Reject unintentional repeats even if their crop or position changed.

## 4. Composition gate

- Preserve source aspect ratio.
- Inspect faces, hands, text, and product geometry at full resolution.
- Check safe margins and every title-card frame.
- Confirm any motion blur disappears when motion stops.

## 5. Delivery gate

- Probe the final file for duration, frame size, frame rate, codecs, and audio.
- Scrub the final encoded render, not only the editor preview.
- Produce a lightweight review copy when the master is too large for convenient playback.
- Keep private data and project-only notes out of distributable files and metadata.
